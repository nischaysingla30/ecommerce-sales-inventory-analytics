The Business Challenge

Retail managers struggle with matching inventory levels to purchasing patterns, leading to stockouts of high-demand items and bloated holding costs for underperforming products. Additionally, high returns cut deep into product margins. This project consolidates raw sales records into actionable visual reports to resolve these operational bottlenecks.

Technologies Demonstrated

  • Python: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib (Data generation, cleaning & EDA)
  • SQL: Joins, CTEs, Windows, Views (PostgreSQL & SQLite friendly)
  • Excel: XLOOKUP, SUMIFS, Pivot Dashboards, formatting
  • Power BI: Star Schema, DAX time-intelligence, interactive dashboards

Data Analytics Pipeline

1

Synthetic Data Generation (Python)

Simulated a multi-table database with anomalies (null signup dates, duplicate line items, negative quantities, and age outliers) using numpy and pandas. The dataset spans Jan 2024 to Dec 2025.

2

Preprocessing & Cleaning (Python)

Deduplicated lines, imputed missing customer values using median age and placeholder genders, capped quantities, and verified referential integrity before exporting clean CSVs.

3

Exploratory Analysis (Python)

Rendered primary visualizations for seasonal sales patterns, category margins, and warehouse reorder alerts using Matplotlib.

4

SQL Relational Warehouse (SQL)

Imported cleaned files into structured tables, setup foreign keys, created unified analytics views, and wrote CTEs/Window functions for advanced metrics.

5

Excel Diagnostics & Dashboard (Excel)

Compiled a formatted workbook featuring VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP columns, conditional formatting alerts, and dynamic pivot-like summary cards.

Calculated Columns & Formulas

The transactional sheets use dynamic references to isolate fields without hardcoding values:

  • Retail Price lookup: =XLOOKUP(C2, Products!$A$2:$A$101, Products!$E$2:$E$101)
  • Net Revenue calculation: =I2-J2 (Gross Revenue - Discount Amount)
  • Item Profit check: =IF(F2=1, -L2, K2-L2) (Evaluates if returned)
  • Category KPI aggregation: =SUMIFS(Order_Items!$K$2:$K$120000, Order_Items!$N$2:$N$120000, A11)

Strategic Insights & Recommendations

📦 Demand & Stock Outages Optimization

Finding: 22% of stock is currently at or below the reorder point. High-volume categories like Electronics face potential stockout during Q4 seasonal spikes.

Recommendation: Set up automated reorder scripts to trigger purchase orders to vendors when inventory crosses the reorder point. Pre-stock key electronic models by mid-September.

💄 Focus on High-Margin Categories

Finding: Although Electronics drives the highest volume, Beauty & Personal Care generates a massive 54% profit margin compared to Electronics' 32.1%.

Recommendation: Shift digital marketing spend weight to Beauty products to optimize net margins. Bundle beauty items with low-margin electronic purchases.

📦 Return Rate Mitigation

Finding: The Clothing category experiences a return rate exceeding 6.2%, causing significant logistics losses.

Recommendation: Redesign sizing descriptions and introduce virtual model tools to set better consumer expectations.

💼 Segment and Region Bundling

Finding: Corporate segments in East and West regions have the highest Average Order Value (AOV) of $210.

Recommendation: Design B2B office equipment bundles containing chargers and stands alongside core laptop purchases to upsell corporate clients.
